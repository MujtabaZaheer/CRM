Comprehensive Education CRM and Student Application Management Platform
The proposed system should be a multi-tenant, role-based Education CRM for managing
student leads, counselling, university applications, documents, partner universities, external
agents, communication, reporting, and operational performance.
1. System Objectives
The web application should enable education consultants, universities, recruitment agencies,
counsellors, students, and external agents to:
· Capture and manage prospective-student leads.
· Convert leads into university applications.
· Track applications through configurable workflows.
· Manage student documents and compliance requirements.
· Maintain university, campus, programme, intake, fee, and eligibility data.
· Coordinate work among counsellors, agents, offices, and teams.
· Communicate with students through email, WhatsApp, SMS, and internal messaging.
· Use AI to analyse documents, identify missing information, recommend programmes,
and prepare applications.
· Generate operational, financial, conversion, and performance reports.
· Maintain security, privacy, auditability, and regulatory compliance.
