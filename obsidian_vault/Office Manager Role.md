# 🏢 Office Manager Role

**Status**: 🟢 Implemented & Live in Production

Manages a branch office, lead distribution, counsellor performance, and office workloads.

## 📋 Responsibilities & Scope (CRM.pdf Section 2 & 3.3)
- **Branch Workload Balancing**: Monitor lead and application queues for assigned branch office.
- **Team Management**: Oversee Team Leaders and Counsellors within the branch.
- **Office KPI Monitoring**: Track office conversion rates, document verification turnaround, and branch revenue goals.
- **Escalation Handling**: Resolve operational bottlenecks between counsellors and admissions officers.
