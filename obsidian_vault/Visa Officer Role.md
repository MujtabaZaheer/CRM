# ✈️ Visa Officer Role

**Status**: 🟢 Implemented & Live in Production (`/visa-officer/*`)

Manages visa application cases, document checklists, embassy appointment tracking, and visa outcome decisions.

## 📋 Responsibilities & Scope (CRM.pdf Section 2 & 3.3)
- **Visa Case Management**: Monitor active visa application cases across status lifecycle (`Preparation`, `Documents Pending`, `Under Review`, `Appointment Scheduled`, `Submitted`, `Granted`, `Refused`, `Escalated`).
- **Visa Document Review**: Review uploaded passport copies, financial bank statements, GTE letters, and health check receipts.
- **Appointment & Deadline Tracking**: Track embassy biometrics and interview appointments.
