# Counsellor Module

Empowers student counsellors to track leads, manage student profiles, monitor applications, upload documents, and match entry requirements.

## 🔗 Connections & Dependencies
- **Role**: [[Counsellor Role]]
- **Data Hook**: `useCounsellorData.ts`
- **Route Guard**: `CounsellorRoute.tsx`

## 📑 Core Entities Handled
- [[Lead Entity]]
- [[Student Entity]]
- [[Application Entity]]
- [[Document Entity]]
- [[Task Entity]]

## 🛠️ Subpages
1. **Dashboard**: Personal lead conversion pipeline.
2. **My Leads**: Lead stage updates and follow-ups.
3. **My Students**: Academic history and English test records.
4. **My Applications**: Draft and submitted applications.
5. **Document Vault**: Document uploads.
6. **Programme Matcher**: Matching student qualifications with university entry criteria.
