# 🏛️ University Partner Portal

**Status**: 🔴 Remaining Roadmap (Phase 3 in CRM.pdf)

Portal for partner university admissions staff to directly log in, review submitted student applications, download document packages, and post decisions.

## 📋 PDF Requirements (CRM.pdf Section 5.3)
- University admissions user login.
- Application queue filtering by intake and programme.
- 1-click offer letter upload and CAS/COE reference entry.
- Direct feedback loop with agency admissions officers.
