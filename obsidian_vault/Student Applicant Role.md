# 🎓 Student Applicant Role

**Status**: 🔴 Remaining Roadmap (Phase 2 in CRM.pdf)

Students who log in to the Student Portal to complete profiles, upload documents, track application milestones, and communicate with counsellors.

## 📋 Responsibilities & Scope (CRM.pdf Section 2 & 3.3)
- **Profile Completion**: Update personal information, academic transcript records, and passport details.
- **Document Self-Upload**: Upload missing transcripts, English test certificates, and SOPs.
- **Milestone Tracking**: View real-time application stage updates, offer letters, deposit requirements, and visa progress.
- **Direct Messaging**: Communicate directly with assigned counsellors.
