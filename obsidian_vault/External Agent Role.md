# 🤝 External Agent Role

**Status**: 🔴 Remaining Roadmap (Phase 2 in CRM.pdf)

Submits and tracks referred student applications, monitors referral status, and views commission statements.

## 📋 Responsibilities & Scope (CRM.pdf Section 2 & 3.3)
- **Referral Submission**: Submit referred student leads and applications via unique tracking links.
- **Application Tracking**: Monitor real-time application stage progress for referred students.
- **Commission Statements**: View earned referral commissions and invoice payout statuses.
