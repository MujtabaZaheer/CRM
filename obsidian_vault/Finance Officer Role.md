# 💳 Finance Officer Role

**Status**: 🟢 Implemented & Live in Production (`/finance/*`)

Manages student fee invoicing, payment receipts, multi-currency transactions, student refund approvals, external agent commissions, and financial reports.

## 📋 Responsibilities & Scope (CRM.pdf Section 2 & 3.3)
- **Invoicing**: Create and issue invoices for application fees, service charges, deposits, tuition fees, and visa support.
- **Payments & Receipts**: Record student payments (Card, Bank Transfer, Cash, Online Gateway, Cheque), generate receipts, and track remaining balances.
- **Refund Management**: Review, approve, or reject student refund claims.
- **Agent Commission Statements**: Track and approve external agent referral commissions (`Pending`, `Eligible`, `Approved`, `Paid`, `Disputed`).
- **Financial Accounting Export**: Export real-time financial statements and accounting data in CSV format.
