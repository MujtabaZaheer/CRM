# 🏢 Organization Admin Role

**Status**: 🟢 Implemented & Live in Production (`/users`, `/audit-log`, etc.)

The **Organization Admin** manages an individual tenant organization, its branch offices, user management, workflows, and master data.

## 📋 Responsibilities & Scope (CRM.pdf Section 2 & 3.3)
- **Organization Structure**: Configure branch offices, teams, and office managers.
- **User Administration**: Create organization users, assign roles, set office permissions, and deactivate accounts.
- **Master Data**: Manage master university & programme catalogs, intake dates, and intake capacity.
- **Workflow & Rules**: Configure lead routing rules, application stage automated transitions, and document requirement templates.
- **Organization Analytics**: High-level reporting on recruitment funnels, counsellor performance, and financial balances.
