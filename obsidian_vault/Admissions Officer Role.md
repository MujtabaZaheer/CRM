# 🛡️ Admissions Officer Role

**Status**: 🟢 Implemented & Live in Production (`/admissions/*`)

Evaluates submitted applications, audits student documents, updates application decisions, issues offer letters, and manages CAS/Visa milestones.

## 📋 Responsibilities & Scope (CRM.pdf Section 2 & 3.3)
- **Application Processing**: Review submitted applications across all **20 lifecycle stages** (`Draft` through `Enrolled`, `Deferred`, `Withdrawn`, `Rejected`).
- **Document Verification Hub**: Audit submitted transcripts, passport validity, and English test certificates with approval/rejection notes.
- **Decision & Offer Issuance**: Issue Conditional and Unconditional offer letters with deposit requirements and conditions.
- **CAS & Visa Milestones**: Track CAS/COE reference numbers, deposit payments, visa document preparation, and enrolment completion.
