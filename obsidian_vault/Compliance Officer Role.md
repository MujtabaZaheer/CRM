# 📜 Compliance Officer Role

**Status**: 🟢 Implemented & Live in Production (`/auditor/*`)

Verifies identity, financial evidence, academic document integrity, and regulatory compliance standards.

## 📋 Responsibilities & Scope (CRM.pdf Section 2 & 3.3)
- **Document Integrity Verification**: Audit student identity evidence, genuine temporary entrant (GTE) proof, and bank financial statements.
- **Regulatory Compliance Audits**: Flag high-risk applications, invalid test scores, or fraudulent document submissions.
- **Compliance Certification**: Record compliance inspection checks (`AUDIT_COMPLIANCE_PASSED` / `AUDIT_COMPLIANCE_FLAGGED`).
