# 🎧 Support User Role

**Status**: 🟢 Implemented & Live in Production (`/support/*`)

Handles technical inquiries, ticket resolution, SLA compliance, knowledge base publishing, and system support.

## 📋 Responsibilities & Scope (CRM.pdf Section 2 & 3.3)
- **Ticket Management**: Resolve technical and operational support tickets (`TKT-2026-XXXX`).
- **Communication Thread**: Provide public user responses and record internal agent notes.
- **Knowledge Base**: Author and publish troubleshooting articles and FAQ guides.
- **SLA Benchmark Monitoring**: Track average resolution hours and manage ticket escalation alerts.
