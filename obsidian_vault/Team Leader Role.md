# 👥 Team Leader Role

**Status**: 🟢 Implemented & Live in Production (`/team-leader/*`)

Oversees team activity, application reassignments, counsellor performance, and operational task tracking.

## 📋 Responsibilities & Scope (CRM.pdf Section 2 & 3.3)
- **Application Reassignment**: Reallocate applications among counsellors based on availability and specialty.
- **Team Activity Monitoring**: Track counsellor daily task completion, student follow-ups, and document uploads.
- **Performance Analytics**: Review counsellor lead conversion rates, application success rates, and turnaround times.
