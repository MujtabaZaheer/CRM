# 🎓 Counsellor Role

**Status**: 🟢 Implemented & Live in Production (`/counsellor/*`)

Frontline student advisor managing recruitment leads, student profiles, document collection, application submission, and programme eligibility matching.

## 📋 Responsibilities & Scope (CRM.pdf Section 2 & 3.3)
- **Lead Pipeline**: Qualify new recruitment leads, update lead stages, record interaction notes, and schedule follow-ups.
- **Student Profile Management**: Convert leads into full student profiles with academic history, GPA, and English proficiency scores (IELTS/TOEFL/PTE/Duolingo/MOI).
- **Document Collection**: Upload required student documents (Passports, Transcripts, Degree Certificates, Recommendation Letters, SOPs).
- **Programme Matcher**: Evaluate student academic qualifications against university admission criteria using the interactive Programme Matcher.
- **Application Draft & Submission**: Prepare applications and submit them to admissions officers.
