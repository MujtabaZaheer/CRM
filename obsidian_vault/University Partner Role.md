# 🏛️ University Partner Role

**Status**: 🔴 Remaining Roadmap (Phase 3 in CRM.pdf)

Admissions representatives at partner universities who review submitted applications and issue direct admission decisions.

## 📋 Responsibilities & Scope (CRM.pdf Section 2 & 3.3)
- **Application Review**: Inspect submitted student applications directly inside the University Portal.
- **Direct Decisions**: Issue conditional/unconditional offers, reject, or request additional documentation.
- **CAS / COE Issuance**: Provide official CAS/COE reference numbers directly to the CRM.
