# Document Entity

Stores student-uploaded verification evidence (Passports, Academic Transcripts, Degree Certificates, IELTS, MOI, Personal Statements, Visa docs).

## 📊 Verification States
- `Received` / `Pending`
- `Verified` (Approved by [[Admissions Officer Module]])
- `Rejected` (With feedback reason for re-upload)
